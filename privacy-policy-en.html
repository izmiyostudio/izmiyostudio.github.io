İzmiyo Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This policy explains how İzmiyo Studio mobile applications process personal data. Some sections may not apply to every application.

1. Data controller and contact

Data controller/operator: İzmiyo Studio

Email: izmiyostudio@gmail.com

2. Applications covered

This policy covers Gönül Rehberi, Ruh Halim, Akıllı Belge, FişCep, İlaç Takibi, UstaDefter, Tadilat Rehberi, Mekanik Hesap Merkezi, Mesleki Hesap Makinesi, Aradığım Nerede, SnapFix, Motorcu Asistanı and Sök-Tak Mekanik Puzzle.

3. Data we may process

  • Email address, user ID and session information when an account is used.
  • Content entered by the user, such as projects, notes, documents, tasks, calculations, mood or medication records.
  • Photos, camera or files when the user explicitly enables the related feature.
  • Device location when the user enables location-based features such as prayer times, Qibla or routes.
  • App settings, notification preferences and technical error information.

4. How data is used

Data is used to provide app features, store records on the device, provide optional account synchronization, respond to requests and maintain security.

5. Local storage and optional accounts

Offline applications primarily store records on the device. Where accounts or synchronization are offered, account creation is optional unless stated otherwise.

6. Permissions

Camera, file, location and notification permissions are requested only when the related feature is used. Permissions can be disabled in device settings.

7. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or rent personal data. Authentication and synchronization applications may use technical service providers to operate those features. If advertising or analytics are enabled, the relevant app listing and this policy will be updated.

8. Retention and deletion

Local records may remain until the user deletes them or removes the app. For account deletion or privacy requests, contact izmiyostudio@gmail.com.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, although no device, network or internet transmission is completely risk-free.

10. Children

Our applications are not designed to knowingly collect personal data from children. Report concerns to izmiyostudio@gmail.com.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated on this page.
